About Us

Royal Energy Industry Co., Ltd. was established with a commitment to the development of palm tree plantations and the cultivation of related agricultural crops. Our operations span the treatment, processing, and marketing of palm products in both raw and processed forms, as well as the production and sale of by-products derived from these activities.

Headquartered in Thailand, we specialize in the production and export of high-quality palm oil and its derivatives. Our core products include:

  • Crude Palm Oil
  • Palm Kernel
  • Seedlings
  • Other agro-allied products

As a trusted name in the industry, we strive to be recognized as a leading exporter of premium palm-based products. Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities operate under rigorous quality control systems and are certified to HACCP, ISO 9001, and Kosher standards—ensuring product safety, consistency, and regulatory compliance.

Beyond supplying products, we offer value-added services and customized solutions tailored to meet the dynamic needs of our global clients. With a strong focus on reliability, quality, and customer satisfaction, Royal Energy Industry Co., Ltd. delivers excellence at every step of the value chain.
