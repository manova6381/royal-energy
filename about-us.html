About Us

Royal Energy Industries Co., Ltd. is a Thailand-based manufacturer and exporter of palm oil and palm-derived products, serving customers across local and international markets.

We combine industry experience, reliable production, and a strong commitment to quality to provide products that meet the needs of a wide range of applications and industries.

What We Do

Our operations cover the production, processing, supply, and export of palm-based products and valuable co-products.

Our product portfolio includes:

  • Crude Palm Oil (CPO)
  • Palm Kernel Oil (PKO)
  • Palm Kernel Shell (PKS)
  • Palm Acid Oil (PAO)
  • Crude Glycerine (CG)
  • Palm Kernel Expeller (PKE)
  • Seedlings and other agro-based products

We work closely with our customers to understand their requirements and provide consistent products, dependable supply, and responsive service.

Quality & Standards

Quality is central to our business. Our operations follow established quality and safety procedures and comply with applicable Thai standards and regulatory requirements.

We maintain careful controls throughout our operations and continuously improve our processes to ensure product consistency, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Why Choose Royal Energy Industries?

  • Consistent Quality: We focus on maintaining reliable product quality and meeting agreed specifications.
  • Reliable Supply: Our production and supply capabilities are designed to support customers with dependable and consistent deliveries.
  • Customer-Focused Service: We work directly with customers to understand their requirements and provide practical solutions for their business.
  • Global Reach: With export capabilities and experience in international markets, we support customers across different regions and industries.
  • Responsible Growth: We aim to grow our business responsibly while improving efficiency, quality, and sustainability throughout our operations.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision: To become a respected global partner in palm-derived products, recognized for quality, reliability, and responsible growth.

Our Mission: To produce and supply high-quality palm-derived products while continuously improving our operations, strengthening customer relationships, and creating long-term value.

Our Commitment

Our business is built around four core principles:

  • Quality: Delivering products that meet customer expectations and specifications.
  • Reliability: Providing dependable supply, service, and communication.
  • Responsibility: Operating with care for our customers, people, resources, and environment.
  • Partnership: Building long-term relationships based on trust, transparency, and mutual growth.
