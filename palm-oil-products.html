Crude Palm Oil
(CPO)

Crude Palm Oil (CPO) is the unrefined oil extracted from the reddish mesocarp (pulp) of the oil palm fruit. Rich in natural carotenoids and tocopherols, CPO serves as a fundamental raw material across the global food, oleochemical, and renewable energy sectors.

Processed under strict quality controls to prevent oxidation and degradation, our CPO offers consistent stability and optimal yield. It serves as the ideal foundation for refining into edible cooking oils, specialty fats, bio-based lubricants, and biodiesel feedstocks.
