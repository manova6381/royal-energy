About Royal Energy Industries Co., Ltd.

Based in Thailand, Royal Energy Industries Co., Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and exporter of high-quality palm oil and palm-derived products, serving customers in global markets.

Our portfolio includes Crude Palm Oil, Palm Kernel Oil, palm-based co-products, and other agro-based solutions. With a strong focus on quality, reliable supply, responsible sourcing, and customer satisfaction, we deliver consistent products and practical solutions tailored to the needs of customers worldwide.
