Crude Palm Oil (CPO)

Refined Glycerine (RG)

A clear, odorless liquid used in pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, food, and industrial products for its moisturizing and stabilizing properties.

Read More

Palm Kernel Oil (PKO)

PKO is extracted from the seed of the palm fruit and is light in color with properties similar to coconut oil. It's commonly used in food products, personal care items, and as a base for soaps and cosmetics.

Read More

Palm Shortenings (PS)

Palm shortening is a semi-solid fat made from refined palm oil. It’s widely used in baking and frying for its stability, long shelf life, and ability to create light, flaky textures in food products.


Read More

Crude Glycerine (CG)

Crude Glycerine is a byproduct of biodiesel and fat processing. It contains glycerol along with impurities and is commonly used in animal feed, industrial applications, and chemical manufacturing.

Read More

Animal Feed (AF)

Our animal feed products are derived from palm and coconut oil byproducts, offering a high-fiber, protein-rich source ideal for livestock and poultry nutrition. Economical, sustainable, and effective for balanced feeding.

Industries we cover

Cosmetics

Palm-derived ingredients offer safe, natural, and effective solutions for cosmetics like lipsticks, eyeshadows, and skincare. They provide excellent performance while supporting clean beauty and sustainable sourcing.

Pharmaceutical / Food

Palm-based derivatives are widely used in food packaging and as efficient emulsifiers in food processing. In pharmaceuticals, they function as emollients and thickening agents, contributing to the stability, texture, and effectiveness of various formulations.

Industrial

Palm-based derivatives are widely used in the automotive and industrial sectors for the formulation of greases and lubricants. These materials deliver reliable lubrication and protection for mechanical components, enhancing equipment performance and longevity.

Household

Palm-based ingredients are essential in household product formulations such as surfactants, cleaners, foam boosters, and degreasers—improving the performance and efficiency of everyday cleaning solutions.
