Palm Kernel Expeller (PKE)

Palm Kernel Expeller (PKE), also known as Palm Kernel Meal, is the solid byproduct generated during the mechanical extraction of oil from the palm kernel. Rich in crude protein, dietary fiber, and essential minerals, PKE serves as an outstanding, energy-dense feed ingredient for livestock, particularly ruminants such as dairy and beef cattle.

Processed without harsh chemical solvents, our PKE retains high digestibility and a balanced amino acid profile. Its high fiber content supports optimal rumen health, while its consistent quality and competitive pricing make it a preferred global choice for commercial animal feed manufacturers and agricultural operations.
